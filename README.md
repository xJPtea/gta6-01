# Coral Bay Heist

An original open-world crime game, built for the make.fun GTA 6 bounty. 100% original: its own city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sound — nothing from any commercial game.

Rae "Reef" Marlowe is a getaway driver who owes the fixer Mama June one last job. Explore Coral Bay on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Coral Bay PD as your wanted level climbs, and run three story missions: deliver a package to the Old Docks, collect three cash drops across town, and survive an ambush by the Salt Crew before reaching the safehouse.

## What's in the game

- A city to explore: a road grid with traffic, pedestrians, parks, a beach and the Old Docks yard
- Cars to steal and drive, with arcade handling
- A wanted level (1–5 stars) with police cars and foot cops who respond to it — get caught on foot and you're BUSTED
- Three missions with objectives on the HUD, plus combat (shoot with the mouse or Space)
- HUD with minimap, money, health, wanted stars and mission distance
- Title screen, pause, BUSTED / WASTED screens, and a save (Q) that loads on the next visit
- Synthesised WebAudio sound: engine, sirens, gunshots, pickups

## Controls

- WASD / arrows — move on foot, drive the car
- E — steal a car / exit a car
- Mouse click / Space — shoot
- Q — save
- ESC — pause
- Enter — start / continue

## Play it

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser, or serve the folder with any static server. No build step, no libraries, no network requests.
